# StayMap Support

StayMap keeps your hotel journal on your device. An optional account adds a private inbox for forwarded confirmations; it does not back up or sync your stays.

## Forward a confirmation

1. In an account-enabled build, open **Settings → Account & forwarding** and sign in with the code emailed to you. Your first sign-in creates an account.
2. Copy your private forwarding address. Forward the confirmation directly to that address as the only recipient, without CC/BCC recipients.
3. Refresh **Forwarded confirmations**, choose **Send to import inbox**, then review the details before saving the stay.

Forwarding sends the complete email, including attachments, to Resend. StayMap imports readable email text. For PDF- or image-only confirmations, share the file or screenshots directly with StayMap instead. Very large emails may also need this local route.

If **Account & forwarding** is missing, your installed build may not include the account service. If a code or confirmation is missing, check the address, check your spam folder for sign-in codes and refresh the inbox. Emails sent to a replaced address cannot reach your current inbox. Accounts and forwarding require an internet connection.

## Manage your information

Use **Get a new address** if your forwarding address has been shared too widely. Anyone who knows it can send mail to your inbox.

**Discard email** removes a message from StayMap's active forwarding inbox. Downloaded copies and saved stays remain. Pending cloud messages normally expire after 30 days at the next daily cleanup; outages can delay removal. Providers retain their own copies under their policies.

Delete your account in **Settings → Account & forwarding → Delete account**, confirming with a fresh email code. This removes the account, forwarding address and active cloud inbox. It leaves your local journal and downloaded import copies in place. Signing out does not delete the account. Neither action cancels a booking.

## Contact

For help, feedback, or bug reports, email **graftonclark@me.com**. Include the app version and what happened. Never send sign-in codes; remove private booking information from screenshots unless it is needed to investigate. Issues posted to this repository are public.

For help accessing or deleting account, support or provider-held information, contact the same address. See the policy for local copies, backups and provider retention.
